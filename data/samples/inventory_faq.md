# Inventory and Stock FAQ (Demo)

This is fictional sample content for the AI Friday workshop.

## How often does store inventory sync?

Store inventory syncs to the central system every 15 minutes. If a store has not synced for more than 6 hours, its stock figures are considered stale and should not be quoted to customers.

## What should I tell a customer when an item is out of stock?

Offer to check the two nearest stores, and offer to place a transfer request. Transfers between stores normally arrive within 2 business days.

## Which items can be transferred between stores?

Over-the-counter items and most prescription medications can be transferred. Controlled substances cannot be transferred between stores without the pharmacy manager's written approval.

## Where do live stock numbers come from?

Live stock numbers come from the inventory search service, not from this FAQ. If the service is unavailable, tell the customer the current stock is unknown and offer to call them back.
